# Copy sources: Protocolo Ofrenda

Every cultural claim shipped in frames 01-05 must appear here with its source. The copy is Spanish; quotes are verbatim from the source. Research date: 2026-10-31.

Status:
- **Verified**: a primary or official source states it.
- **Reworded**: verified, but the copy must keep the wording given here (usually "se cree que").
- **Do not ship**: only press or secondary sources, or the sources disagree.

Preferred sources are UNESCO and Mexican government pages (gob.mx, Secretaría de Cultura, SIDOF, state governments). The INBA sites (inba.gob.mx, museomuraldiegorivera.inba.gob.mx) refused connections on the research date, so the Secretaría de Cultura press release covers the mural.
